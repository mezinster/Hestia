Version 2.5.0. Important fix: creating or editing a schedule whose slot already covers the current time only turned the outlet on inside the app, never for real — fixed, the outlet now switches on right away. Dashboard: shows how long an outlet has been drawing power under "On", and the configured cutoff threshold now shows up even without a countdown. New colors to better tell Presence and Schedule apart, matching the Detail screen. The on/off button, during a presence simulation, now simply stops it instead of starting something else in its place.
