Version 2.6.0. The physical button and presence simulation no longer fight each other: during an active presence window, pressing the button turns the outlet on without arming a competing timer, and stopping presence "for today" immediately unblocks the button instead of presence cutting it back off. Also fixes a bug that could wrongly disable a freshly created presence or schedule on a channel that had seen a button press before. The save icon now appears on "Save" (Edit device), a checkmark confirms a click was registered while saving a schedule (prevents duplicates from a double-click), and fixes an unnecessary empty band above the Dashboard's bottom tabs. Export/import: the "no duration limit" custom preset setting is now properly saved and restored.
