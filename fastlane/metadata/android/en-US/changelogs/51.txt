Version 2.14.0. Two-tap ergonomics: tapping any outlet (single or in a multi-outlet block) now opens a quick modal to launch a program (1h/2h/3h, a saved preset, or Manual) right away, without going through the detail screen — now renamed "Configure", it is used only for settings (create/edit a preset, schedule, button timer, cutoff…). The modal's on/off button now reflects the outlet's real state (green background when on). Modal: the text shown during an active schedule or presence simulation no longer made sense ("Scheduled for another 14:00 - 17:00") — fixed. Settings: a multi-outlet block's icon now uses the connectivity indicator instead of a grid icon; the Backup section has moved to the bottom of the screen.
